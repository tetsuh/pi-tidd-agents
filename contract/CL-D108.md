## CL-D108 — A trusted timeline correction settles a body claim
**Clauses:** CL-D108-record, CL-D108-block, CL-D108-tests, CL-D108-forward-d95

*Decision ID:* CL-D108
*Kind:* contract
*Target and revision:* `tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents#242` at its body, the design proposal https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/242#issuecomment-6048800736, and the owner decision https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/242#issuecomment-6054014308
*Question:* Corrections to a pull request go in its timeline, never its body (an owner rule for this loop). On PR #239 round 5 and PR #255 round 1, convergence read a timeline comment that corrected a body sentence and still raised a blocking finding asking for the body itself to change, while Sol accepted the same kind of correction on PR #236. Whether a stale body blocked depended on the anchoring a gate chose. How does a gate judge a body claim that the timeline has corrected?
*Options and trade-offs:* Option A states once, in the shared Every-gate block, that a body claim a trusted conversation comment corrects is judged as corrected, unless the correction is itself inaccurate. Option B anchors every body-only finding `reword`, so CL-D85 records it and it never blocks, which reuses existing machinery but lets a false body claim with no correction anywhere through.
*Recommendation:* Option A.
*Owner choice:* Option A. The shared Every-gate invariant payload block carries the sentence after CL-D95's, so every gate payload of both roots carries it: a pull-request body claim that a later conversation comment by a trusted author (owner, member or collaborator, not a bot) corrects is judged as corrected; the gate cites that comment and raises no finding against the body sentence, unless the correction itself is inaccurate against the head. An uncorrected body claim is judged as CL-D95 says.
*Rationale:* The comment is where the workflow puts the correction. Each PR gate's required evidence names the pull request's snapshot, whose conversation comments carry each author's association and type, and the gate roles read them with their read-only `bash`; a finding that asks for the body to repeat it costs a round and an owner decision and changes nothing a reader needs.
*Validity and invalidation conditions:* Applies to every gate invocation in both workflow roots. It changes no verdict rule, no schema field and no driver; a gate role definition under `agents/` is unchanged. Letting a correction by an untrusted author or a bot settle a claim, or settling a claim the correction misstates, requires a new owner decision.
