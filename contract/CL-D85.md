## CL-D85 — Wording-only Minors do not stop a run
**Clauses:** CL-D85-classes, CL-D85-readiness, CL-D85-routing, CL-D85-order, CL-D85-gates, CL-D85-misses, CL-D85-status, CL-D85-record, CL-D85-tests

*Decision ID:* CL-D85
*Kind:* contract
*Target and revision:* `tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents#153` at its body, the owner decision of 2026-09-20 recorded there, and the owner choice on the guard reset https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/153#issuecomment-5777454006 (working session of 2026-09-22)
*Question:* PR #147 took six review rounds to reach `MERGE_READY` and rounds 2 to 6 changed only prose. Each of those rounds cost a full set of gate invocations, and each invalidated every gate's evidence. Two mechanisms produced it: a Minor that changes no file of the head still stopped the run, and the round chronology lived in the pull-request body, so every round edited the body and moved the snapshot it had just been reviewed against. What stops a run, and what does the body carry?
*Options and trade-offs:* Option A records the three rules: a Minor whose correction changes no file of the head is recorded rather than blocking, the chronology leaves the body for the timeline, and a repeat of a settled counterexample class is recorded as the earlier round's review miss. It ends the body-drift loop and keeps every finding visible, at the cost of a readiness report that may carry recorded Minors. Option B keeps the rules and raises the round limit, which buys rounds without removing the mechanism that consumes them. Option C makes wording findings unreportable, which loses the finding rather than the round.
*Recommendation:* Option A.
*Owner choice:* Option A (2026-09-20, recorded in the issue body). In a pull-request run, a Minor whose correction changes no file of the head, or changes only wording that alters no obligation — no sentence the clause manifest pins and no behaviour a test asserts — is recorded with its anchoring class and its disposition, the run proceeds to the next gate, and readiness may be reported with such Minors recorded. A gate result whose only open findings are Minors recorded under CL-D85 advances as `MERGE` at every transition — convergence to Sol, Sol to Terra, and Terra to the final check — and is not a correction path; those findings stay recorded and dispositioned, and the head does not move for them. A finding that corrects a false safety claim changes what the contract promises, so it is not wording only and keeps its severity. The chronology of review rounds leaves the pull-request body for the target's timeline; the body keeps the `Closes` line, the Scope paragraph, and the AC-TDD classification, so a round edits no body and invalidates no snapshot by doing so. When a gate raises a finding of the same counterexample class as one the settled ledger already carries for an earlier head of the same pull request, the run also records it in its terminal report, and in review-only in the status block's `review_misses`, as a review miss of the gate and invocation that did not raise it, which is a metric the owner tracks and not a change of the finding's own disposition. The addendum's recorded guard resets from 29,000 to 32,000 bytes and the authority ceiling from 150,000 to 156,000 bytes for the three rules recorded here, on the terms CL-D43, CL-D48, and CL-D74 set: the headroom is asserted at the raise against the measurement taken when it was chosen — 29,776 and 149,602 bytes at `f71077f`, leaving 2,224 and 6,398 — the figures are revision-qualified rather than running totals, no prose was trimmed to make room, and both helper alarms are untouched (owner choice, https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/153#issuecomment-5780064415, superseding the 30,000 chosen at https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/153#issuecomment-5777454006 before the routing corrections were known). CL-D104 later raised the authority ceiling to 162,000 bytes on the same terms.
*Rationale:* The round is the expensive unit: it re-runs every gate and invalidates the evidence they produced. Spending one on a change that no gate can observe in the head is the cost of treating every Minor as a stop, and the body chronology guaranteed that at least one such change existed per round. Recording the finding and continuing keeps what review is for — the finding is still reported and still dispositioned — while the review miss makes the repeat visible as a property of the review rather than only of the pull request.
*Validity and invalidation conditions:* Applies to findings, readiness, and the pull-request body under both modes. No grant widens: exact autofix still takes one commit and one push per correction batch and no body edit, and review-only still publishes nothing itself. The severity vocabulary, the anchoring classes, and the disposition vocabulary are unchanged; what changes is that a recorded Minor of this kind does not stop the run. Existing pull-request bodies are not rewritten; the three-part body applies from the next pull request. Treating a `Blocker` or `Major` as wording only, reporting readiness with an undispositioned finding, or returning the chronology to the body requires a new owner decision. The packaged validator does not yet represent this class: `checkVerdict` treats a fresh Minor with an anchoring class and a no-code disposition as unresolved, so the gate that first raises one returns `FIX BEFORE MERGE` and only a later invocation of that gate, after the parent assigns its `blockerKey` and the gate confirms the disposition, can return `MERGE`. Until that is decided (Issue #172), the recorded Minor still never blocks readiness and the chronology still never moves the body; what is not yet saved is the invocation that first raises it. CL-D95 later let a repository's own pull-request template govern the body's sections, so the three parts named here apply where the repository has none.
