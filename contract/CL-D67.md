## CL-D67 — Pull-request bodies carry no per-head facts
**Clauses:** CL-D67-template, CL-D67-record, CL-D67-tests

*Decision ID:* CL-D67
*Kind:* contract
*Target and revision:* `tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents#120` at its body, the design comment https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/120#issuecomment-5600017960, and the owner choice https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/120#issuecomment-5602812931
*Question:* Three PR #113 review rounds were body-only findings: the body restated repository facts or per-head measurements and drifted from the head. Under exact autofix the writer cannot correct a body, because the CL-D30 grant is one commit and one push, so such a finding stops the run at `WAITING_FOR_OWNER`. Where do per-head evidence and repository facts live, and does the grant need a bounded body edit?
*Options and trade-offs:* Option A adds one bounded pull-request body edit to the exact-autofix grant, a PATCH of one delimited evidence block from packaged measurements; it keeps the body self-describing at the cost of a new GitHub mutation in the grant, a new builder and guard, addendum bytes, a new failure class when the PATCH races a human edit, and a new decision each time the block shape changes. Option B keeps per-head facts out of bodies: the PR Skill states the body template, and measurements live in commit messages and in the review's published comment. Option C keeps a human updating the body each head, which leaves exact autofix stopping on body findings.
*Recommendation:* Option B.
*Owner choice:* Option B. The PR Skill states the body template: a `Closes` line with the owner-decision link, a Scope paragraph pointing to the contract record, the manifest clauses, and the carrying files, the AC-TDD classification of the RED, and a chronology of review rounds. Per-head measurements live in commit messages and in the review's published comment, never in the body; the exact-autofix publication grant is unchanged: one commit and one push per correction batch, no body edit.
*Rationale:* The drift class exists only because bodies restated what the repository and the review already carry; removing the restatement removes the class, while Option A would add mutation authority to repair it. The review's published comment already carries exact-head validation and authority figures measured by the run, and a commit message is immutable evidence bound to its head. PR #113 and #116 moved to this template before the decision and produced no further body findings.
*Validity and invalidation conditions:* Applies to pull-request bodies under this workflow and to what the gates may treat as a body claim. It changes no verdict vocabulary, gate authority, or budget. Adding a body edit to the grant requires a new owner decision; a criterion that demands a measurement in the body itself is satisfied by the commit message and the review comment under this record. CL-D85 later moved the fourth part, the round chronology, out of the body to the target's timeline, so the body carries three parts and a review round edits none of them; the grant is unchanged by that move, and existing bodies are not rewritten. CL-D95 later let a repository's own pull-request template govern the sections in place of the three parts, keeping the rules on per-head facts and chronology.
