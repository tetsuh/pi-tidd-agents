## CL-D51 — A zero-output gate transport failure may relaunch once
**Clauses:** CL-D51-mapping, CL-D51-tests

*Decision ID:* CL-D51
*Kind:* contract
*Target and revision:* `tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents#82` at its initial body and the owner's live selection of **Option A**
*Question:* May a gate invocation that failed with zero designated output bytes and no parsable content be relaunched exactly once in the same run, before the failure becomes terminal?
*Options and trade-offs:* Option A permits one relaunch under the strictest observable precondition; Option B keeps every transport failure terminal, discarding preflight, workspace, snapshot, and fingerprints for a failure that carried no information about the target — the observed cost was roughly 150 whole-run losses with no repository or GitHub mutation pending in any of them. A trusts one narrowly checkable fact; B trusts nothing and pays for it every time.
*Recommendation:* Option A.
*Owner choice:* Option A. A gate child that dies of a pure transport failure with **zero designated output bytes** may be relaunched exactly once per run as `gate_transport@gate_launch`, consuming no counter, with identity, every applicable fingerprint, and both live invariants freshly re-proved first. Any designated output byte, however malformed, keeps the failure terminal; after any mutation, Luna task, or reply the key is terminal exactly as before.
*Rationale:* CL-D39 names widening the recoverable set as requiring a new owner decision; this is that decision, and it inherits CL-D39's own preconditions rather than inventing weaker ones. Zero output is the dividing line because it is the strongest checkable absence: with no bytes there is no partial verdict to misread, double-count, or contradict, so a relaunch can conflict with nothing the failed attempt produced — where a child that emitted anything at all may have acted on the world or produced content whose meaning is disputed, and stays terminal. The structured-output transport makes the condition observable: the designated output file either exists with bytes or it does not. The relaunch replaces the failed invocation with the same prevalidated one, which is CL-D39's replacement discipline applied to the one case where the replacement is identical by construction. One per run, not per gate, keeps the budget below every counter it could interact with.
*Validity and invalidation conditions:* Applies to exact PR `autofix` gate launches before any mutation, Luna task, or reply. It grants no retry for partial, malformed, or ambiguous output, no second relaunch, no counter relief, and no change to `reply_outcome_unknown` or any publication one-shot. If the gate transport ever stops making zero-output observably distinct from partial output, the dividing line recorded here is invalidated. Widening further — a second relaunch, partial-output tolerance, or another phase — requires a new owner decision. CL-D58 later fixed where the designated output is read: the run's structured output path, never a completion notice.
