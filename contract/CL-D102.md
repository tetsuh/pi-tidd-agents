## CL-D102 — A gate reports every finding it can establish in one invocation
**Clauses:** CL-D102-record, CL-D102-block, CL-D102-tests

*Decision ID:* CL-D102
*Kind:* contract
*Target and revision:* `tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents#228` at its body and the owner's go-ahead of 2026-10-04 in the working session ("file both and proceed")
*Question:* A gate returned one finding per run. Each correction moves the head, so each finding cost a full round: a validation, an evidence collection, and up to three gate runs. PR #227 took five rounds with one finding each, three of them stopped by convergence; tetsuh/hekatus#90 took four runs, each stopped by convergence with one different Minor, and never reached Sol. No gate role, and nothing in the gate contract, told a gate to stop at its first finding or to report all it can establish. What does an invocation report?
*Options and trade-offs:* Option 1 leaves it to the model, which is what produced one finding per run. Option 2 states the rule once for every gate in the shared Every-gate block, which every Issue and PR gate payload carries, and in the three PR gate roles' working rules: report every finding the invocation can establish, and after a finding check the rest of the target for the same class. A longer invocation, within the unchanged launch bound of CL-D94, is the cost; a round saved is a validation, an evidence collection and up to three gate runs.
*Recommendation:* Option 2.
*Owner choice:* Option 2 (https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/228). The shared Every-gate invariant payload block carries the sentence, so every packaged gate payload carries it, convergence included. `agents/tidd-convergence-reviewer.md`, `agents/tidd-adversarial-reviewer.md` and `agents/tidd-safety-reviewer.md` carry the rule among their working rules; this widens CL-D1's freeze on `agents/` (CL-D35) for that one line in each of the three definitions, and the decision-drift role receives the rule through the payload block alone. The rule changes no verdict rule, no schema field, and no cap: a gate still returns one verdict, a finding still needs its evidence and its anchor, and the round and push limits are as they were.
*Rationale:* The pre-push sweeps of PR #227 showed the effect from the other side: a reviewer told to enumerate a class returned several items at once, and the class did not come back.
*Validity and invalidation conditions:* Applies to every gate invocation in both workflow roots. Telling a gate to stop at its first finding, or removing the rule from the shared block or a gate role, requires a new owner decision.
