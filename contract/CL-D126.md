## CL-D126 — A body-claim counterexample is a Minor reword corrected in the timeline
**Clauses:** CL-D126-record, CL-D126-anchor, CL-D126-every-gate, CL-D126-sol, CL-D126-classes, CL-D126-validator, CL-D126-review, CL-D126-readme, CL-D126-forward, CL-D126-tests

*Decision ID:* CL-D126
*Kind:* contract
*Target and revision:* `tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents#322` at its body, and the owner decision on it (https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/322#issuecomment-6542449273)
*Question:* On tetsuh/hekatus PR #160 review-only ran four rounds; three of the four Majors were counterexamples to a sentence of the pull-request body, not to any acceptance criterion of its Issue, and every one carried `anchoring: criterion-anchored` with AC1 as its anchor, so each round stopped `WAITING_FOR_OWNER`, the author corrected the code, and the next round found the next input the sentence did not survive. CL-D34 already made a body-only claim a `reword` finding, but its text did not say how to anchor a counterexample that concerns a criterion's feature at an input the criterion's text does not reach, the rule reached Sol only (the Sol-only payload block; the Every-gate block and the agent files carry no definition), a `reword` could be Major, which the driver does not record, and its correction was "an owner edit of the body" although corrections go in the timeline (CL-D108). How does a gate anchor a counterexample that falsifies a body claim but no criterion, and what is the correction of such a finding?
*Options and trade-offs:* Option A changes the anchoring text only: a finding is `criterion-anchored` only when the named text reaches the cited input, a counterexample it does not reach that falsifies a body sentence is `reword` whatever feature it concerns, and the correction is a trusted timeline comment; a Major reword still stops the run. Option B adds a bounded severity: a pull-request-root `reword` is Minor only, so CL-D85 records it and the run proceeds with the finding listed in the published round. Option C, a driver check of the anchor, is not possible: whether a criterion's text reaches an input is a judgment, not a string match.
*Recommendation:* Option B.
*Owner choice:* Option B, with three trade-offs read as follows. CL-D108's rejected case is accepted: a pull-request body claim that no criterion, clause or invariant reaches and that nobody corrects reaches `MERGE_READY`; the published round lists the finding and the narrowing it asks for, and the owner reads it before merging. CL-D85's sentence on a false safety claim is unchanged: a body claim that a contract clause or fail-stop invariant reaches is `criterion-anchored` by the reach test and keeps its severity; only a claim none of the three reaches is a Minor reword. CL-D110's carry is narrowed for the review-only driver: its recorded reword line is not carried as settled; the next run judges the claim afresh, and a trusted conversation comment that narrows the claim settles it (CL-D108). The Every-gate payload block carries the reach test and the correction route, so convergence, Sol and Terra receive them; the anchoring section and the Sol-only block say the same; the gate roles' agent definitions change only in the output schema they transcribe (CL-D90). The validator and the version-2 pull-request schema admit a `reword` finding at `Minor` only, so a Major reword is a finding-record failure the driver relaunches once and then ends `BLOCKED`; the Issue root keeps `Major`, because there the body is the specification, and version 1 stays verbatim. The review-only driver publishes a recorded reword as `<disposition> (reword, recorded under CL-D85; judged afresh next run, CL-D126)`: it is a Minor recorded under CL-D85 for every rule that counts those, and a line the earlier-rounds reader does not match as settled. A reword a gate confirms on an assigned finding as accepted as designed is published as `accepted-as-designed (reword, confirmed by <gate>; judged afresh next run, CL-D126)`; a confirmed reword proposed `fixed` is not settled and takes the recorded form. Either line opens its parenthesis with `reword,`, which is what the earlier-rounds reader's settled pattern does not match, so the reader carries neither line. Under the closed disposition vocabulary, `fixed` on a `reword` finding means that conversation comment. The Issue root is unchanged.
*Rationale:* The owner's cost was the stop, not the label: under Option A a correctly labelled reword Major still ends the round and asks for an owner action, while the loop's own rules already say the body is not the specification, corrections go in the timeline, and the owner merges by hand after reading the round. Harnesses that check description claims treat an unsupported claim as a note on the claim, not as a defect of the code. The reach test is the part of the rule the hekatus gates lacked: AC1 read as "the status guard works" anchored every parser edge to it. Narrowing the carry keeps the finding visible across runs until the claim is narrowed, which the published round alone would not.
*Validity and invalidation conditions:* Applies to the pull-request root's gates, schema, validator and review-only driver. The autofix driver's ledger line and its accounting across fresh runs are unchanged and stay with #50: a reword line the autofix driver publishes still reads `(recorded under CL-D85)` and a later review-only run carries it. Raising a pull-request-root reword above `Minor`, correcting a reword by a body edit or a code change, carrying a recorded reword line as settled, or applying the bound to the Issue root requires a new owner decision.
