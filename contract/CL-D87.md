## CL-D87 — The package ships gpt-6-sol and gpt-6-luna
**Clauses:** CL-D87-record, CL-D87-readme, CL-D87-tests

*Decision ID:* CL-D87
*Kind:* contract
*Target and revision:* `tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents#174` at its body and the owner choices https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/174#issuecomment-5788131197 and https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/174#issuecomment-5788195445 (working session of 2026-09-23)
*Question:* `gpt-6-sol` and `gpt-6-luna` are available. The owner already runs them through user-level `agentOverrides`, which needs nothing from the package. What defaults does the package ship to everyone else, and what happens to the Terra roles?
*Options and trade-offs:* Keep `gpt-5.6-*` and leave the move to each deployment, which costs nothing and ships an older generation. Move the Sol and Luna roles and keep `gpt-5.6-terra` for the two Terra roles, which keeps each root's two formal gates on different model families. Move all five, putting the Terra roles on `gpt-6-sol`, which ships one current reviewer model and gives up model-family difference between the formal gates. For the thinking level: keep `high` everywhere, or revisit it per role. For evidence: ship on the owner's word, or first require a qualifying review-only and exact-autofix round under the overrides.
*Recommendation:* Move the Sol and Luna roles and keep Terra, `high` everywhere, ship on the owner's word.
*Owner choice:* All five move (https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/174#issuecomment-5788195445, revising https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/174#issuecomment-5788131197). `tidd-adversarial-reviewer`, `tidd-safety-reviewer`, and `tidd-drift-reviewer` ship `gpt-6-sol`; `tidd-convergence-reviewer` and `tidd-autofix-worker` ship `gpt-6-luna`; every role keeps thinking `high`; no qualifying run is required first. The Terra roles now run the Sol model, so the formal gates of each root no longer differ by model family: they stay separate in the ways that never depended on the model — their own role blocks, and Terra never grading or repairing Sol's verdict; the adversarial and safety gates run in fresh context, while the Issue drift gate keeps the forked context CL-D59 gives it on purpose — and the model-family difference CL-D29 names as the separate agent's primary benefit now separates the gates from the writer rather than from each other. The writer stays on a model no formal gate runs, so CL-D3's self-grading exclusion still holds; the non-authoritative convergence stage does run it, the model-level self-review #102 tracks. Role names, aliases, gate identities, and the envelope are unchanged: CL-D60 already keeps model families out of gate identities.
*Rationale:* A deployment that does nothing should get the current generation. The Terra roles have not stopped a run in the measured history, and the owner prefers one current reviewer model to keeping an older family only for difference's sake. Recording the loss of model-family difference as a choice, in the records that reasoned from it, keeps those records true rather than silently stale.
*Validity and invalidation conditions:* Applies to the shipped agent definitions and every surface that names their defaults. It grants nothing: role authority, read-only and writer resolution, preflight, and every guard are unchanged, and a deployment's `agentOverrides` still take precedence. Records of past runs keep the models those runs used. Shipping a different model for any role, changing a thinking level, or restoring model-family difference between the formal gates requires a new owner decision. CL-D98 later moved the three gpt-6-sol roles to `gpt-6.1-sol`; the Luna roles and every thinking level are unchanged.
