## CL-D56 — Package-owned builders construct the documents the boundary checks
**Clauses:** CL-D56-map, CL-D56-tests

*Decision ID:* CL-D56
*Kind:* contract
*Target and revision:* `tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents#83` at the design comment https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/83#issuecomment-5466322905 and the owner scope decision https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/83#issuecomment-5467176488
*Question:* Should the package construct the documents its boundary checks, and in what surface shape?
*Options and trade-offs:* One CLI operation per builder with a `build_` prefix versus a generic `build` operation with a document discriminator: the generic form reintroduces classification exactly where CL-D44 chose validation, so the family is one CLI operation per builder, each with its own exact-set input freeze. Including `build_gate_expectation` versus freezing scope to the four compositions in the issue body: the owner chose inclusion, because the parent-assembled gate schema was the only builder-class failure observed after filing.
*Recommendation:* One operation per builder, five builders including the gate expectation.
*Owner choice:* As recommended, per the linked scope decision. The correctness mechanism is the validation-not-classification principle applied to construction: each builder passes its own output through the boundary's predicate table (or, for the gate expectation, the boundary's own `expectedState`, correlation schema walk, and required-evidence check) before returning it, so a builder output the boundary would reject is unrepresentable and the predicate table stays the single source of truth. Rejections carry the boundary's vocabulary at phase `build` and name the failed predicate, nothing else.
*Rationale:* CL-D44 shipped the rejection side and left construction unaddressed; in the field every hand-assembly mistake cost a whole non-resumable run (Issue #83's ledger: envelope-for-data swaps, forbidden record fields, hand-computed heads, hand-copied prefixes, and finally a malformed parent-assembled gate schema that killed a run twice). Builders starve the re-derivation anti-pattern by making the packaged derivation cheaper than the hand-rolled one. The builders enter the CLI as new operations with their own frozen inputs; the CL-D44 five-field freeze is untouched, and `freshFindingIdPrefix` remains underivable by hand through the builder exactly as CL-D47 requires.
*Validity and invalidation conditions:* Applies to the five compositions named in the design comment. Builders grant no authority, perform no I/O, and change no operation's semantics, schema, or rejection behavior; the consuming operation revalidates every request as before, so a builder is a convenience with proof, never a bypass. Adding a builder, widening one's inputs, or accepting a construction the boundary would reject requires a new owner decision. If the predicate table and the builders ever validate differently, the executed round-trip fixtures invalidate this record. CL-D61 later added the two `manifest_compare` builders under its own decision.
