## CL-D95 — A repository's own pull-request template governs the body's sections
**Clauses:** CL-D95-template, CL-D95-gate, CL-D95-forward-d67, CL-D95-forward-d85, CL-D95-record, CL-D95-tests

*Decision ID:* CL-D95
*Kind:* contract
*Target and revision:* `tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents#201` at its body and the triage https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/201#issuecomment-5900278630
*Question:* CL-D67 fixes a three-part pull-request body and says nothing about a repository that mandates its own template. On `tetsuh/sitos`, whose `CONTRIBUTING.md` and `AGENTS.md` require a template of eight sections, the two cannot both hold: Sol stopped sitos#187 at `WAITING_FOR_OWNER` on the layout alone, while sitos#185 with the same template reached `MERGE_READY`. Which contract governs the body's sections there, and may a gate raise the layout?
*Options and trade-offs:* Option A lets the repository's own template govern the sections, keeps CL-D67's per-head rule where the template leaves the wording to the author, and makes layout alone never a finding; one sentence in the template section and one in the every-gate payload block, so every gate reads the same rule. Option B keeps CL-D67 everywhere and asks the owner for an exception on every such pull request, which is the stop sitos#187 recorded. Option C records per repository which body contract applies, which settles the choice once but needs a place and a format to hold it.
*Recommendation:* Option A, with Option C deferred (the triage).
*Owner choice:* Proposals 1 and 2 of the issue, agreed in the owner-approved triage (https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/201#issuecomment-5900278630) and recorded here as Option A, which the owner then approved as prepared (https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/201#issuecomment-5909723266); proposal 3, Option C, was deferred there. Where the target repository has a pull-request template of its own — a template file GitHub applies, or a template its `CONTRIBUTING.md` or `AGENTS.md` requires — that template governs the body's sections instead of CL-D67's three parts, and CL-D67's rules on per-head facts and chronology still apply wherever the template leaves the wording to the author. The every-gate payload block states that a body's section layout alone is never a finding and that its claims are judged for accuracy against the head. Recording per repository which body contract applies is deferred until the precedence proves ambiguous.
*Rationale:* The target repository's rules bind its contributors, this workflow included; a workflow template that overrides them makes every compliant pull request a finding. CL-D67's purpose, a body with no claim that depends on the head, survives inside any template that leaves the wording to the author.
*Validity and invalidation conditions:* Applies to the pull-request body's sections. CL-D67's per-head rule, the exact-autofix grant, and every gate's authority are unchanged, and a repository with no template of its own keeps the three parts. A body claim that is false at the head remains a finding under any template. Recording a per-repository choice, or letting a repository template relax the per-head rule where it does not require the fact, requires a new owner decision.
