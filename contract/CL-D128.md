## CL-D128 — Only the snapshot fields a gate can act on invalidate its result
**Clauses:** CL-D128-record, CL-D128-driver, CL-D128-contract, CL-D128-forward, CL-D128-tests

*Decision ID:* CL-D128
*Kind:* contract
*Target and revision:* `tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents#328` at its body, and the owner decision on it (https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/328#issuecomment-6551087484)
*Question:* Before a later gate, before the autofix writer and at final readiness, both drivers took a fresh external snapshot, and any change of its fingerprint cleared every gate verdict and reran convergence (DEC-109-CONV-SNAPSHOT-001, ADV-199-SNAPSHOT-INVALIDATION). A gate payload carries the snapshot only as its fingerprint, so a rerun after a check completed reviewed the same body, diff, criteria and history again. On tetsuh/sitometron PR #114, 11 of the 33 kept runs reran convergence on an unchanged head; in all 11 check runs had completed, in 9 a `Bot` had posted a quality-gate comment, and no human comment, review or thread was new; in 6 the rerun turned a `MERGE` into `FIX BEFORE MERGE`. Which snapshot changes invalidate a gate result?
*Options and trade-offs:* Option A splits the snapshot: the fields a gate can act on invalidate, and the rest, which no gate payload carries, does not. Option B waits, bounded, for checks to settle before the first gate: wall time on every run, and a late check still reruns. Option C reruns convergence on the new events only, keeping its earlier `MERGE` unless they produce a finding: the largest change.
*Recommendation:* Option A.
*Owner choice:* Option A. A change in the target brackets, the pull request apart from its `mergeable` and `mergeable_state`, reviews, review threads, inline review comments, or conversation comments whose author type is not `Bot` clears every gate verdict and reruns convergence, as DEC-109 states. A change confined to check runs, commit statuses, check suites, annotations, branch and ruleset policies, mergeability, or conversation comments by a `Bot` clears no verdict and reruns nothing. Reactions are not review content and are left out. Final readiness still evaluates the final policy on a snapshot read at that moment, so a failing or pending check, an unresolved thread or a non-mergeable state stops the run as before. A gate result still records the full snapshot fingerprint it observed, in its correlation and its required evidence; only the decision to invalidate reads the narrower digest, which the driver keeps beside the fingerprints in the run's state. Both drivers take the rule from the shared snapshot reader; a run whose saved state predates the digest compares the full fingerprint once, as before.
*Rationale:* A rerun that receives the same inputs adds no evidence; it samples convergence again, and on PR #114 the second sample decided six runs. No gate payload carries the excluded fields: a gate receives the snapshot only as its fingerprint, and Sol receives only the linked issue's trusted human comments, read once at the start of the run. The final policy reads what it needs of them, the checks, statuses, policies and `mergeable_state`, at final readiness, so nothing a gate could act on is lost, and a human's comment, review or thread still restarts the sequence.
*Validity and invalidation conditions:* Applies to the review-only and the exact autofix driver on the pull-request root. Adding a field to the invalidating digest that no gate payload carries, dropping one a gate acts on, or letting a check result clear a verdict requires a new owner decision.
