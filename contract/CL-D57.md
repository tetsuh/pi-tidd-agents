## CL-D57 — The batch-sequence guards are packaged and the alarm is reset for them
**Clauses:** CL-D57-map, CL-D57-addendum, CL-D57-tests

*Decision ID:* CL-D57
*Kind:* contract
*Target and revision:* `tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents#96` rule 1, at the design comment https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/96#issuecomment-5477968941 and the owner decision https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/96#issuecomment-5478036060
*Question:* Should Luna's batch-sequence guards become packaged read-only operations, and how is the CL-D37 aggregate smoke alarm handled for that measured, planned growth?
*Options and trade-offs:* Option A packages all four guards and records a CL-D53-style reviewed reset of the aggregate alarm from 160,000 to 200,000 in the same change; Option B packages only the two repeatedly fatal checks and leaves `guard_before_edit` hand-rolled inside the alarm; Option C splits the alarm per concern. Five PR #94 runs died at three distinct hand-rolled guard defects while every authorized correction was correct, so leaving any guard hand-rolled preserves a known killer; the split adds structure the surface does not yet need.
*Recommendation:* Option A.
*Owner choice:* Option A, per the linked decision. Four operations — `guard_before_edit`, `overlay_freeze`, `overlay_compare`, `manifest_compare` — whose failures name the violated subcheck and the observed value, satisfying CL-D55 by construction. Their predicates encode the recorded failure classes: authorized paths are a maximum set, not a demand, and the index is compared against the immutable staged manifest, never against only the authorized changed files. The aggregate smoke alarm resets from 160,000 to 200,000 bytes; the 30,000-byte per-file alarm and every structural boundary assertion are unchanged. The three guard cross-operation fields — `guard_before_edit.expected`, `overlay_compare.overlay`, and `manifest_compare.manifest` — are registered in the CL-D44 pre-dispatch input-shape table under this decision, the optional manifest field validated only when supplied, which is exactly the new-owner-decision route the CL-D44 freeze names for a field addition.
*Rationale:* The three defect classes were all re-derivations of prose-specified checks — an index compared against the wrong baseline, an exact-set assertion where the contract states a maximum set, and a monolithic script whose failure named nothing. Packaging changes who implements the check, not what happens when it truly fails: a true failure keeps its CL-D39 consequences, terminal after editing begins, with no retry and no guard substitution. The alarm handling follows CL-D53's own instruction that the next firing demands the same review; this decision performs that review before the growth instead of after the fire, with the growth measured and itemized rather than discovered.
*Validity and invalidation conditions:* Applies to exact PR autofix's batch-sequence boundaries. The guards are read-only observations and grant no mutation, commit, push, or provider authority; `workspace_verify`'s scope is not reassigned, and `guard_before_edit` composes it rather than replacing it. Widening a guard's tolerance, adding a guard, or accepting an unnamed failure requires a new owner decision. If the alarm fires at 200,000 the CL-D37 review recurs; nothing here converts it into a budget.
