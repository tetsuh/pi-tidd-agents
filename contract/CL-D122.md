## CL-D122 — A thin packaged driver runs the Issue review-and-draft round
**Clauses:** CL-D122-record, CL-D122-driver, CL-D122-skill, CL-D122-trim

*Decision ID:* CL-D122
*Kind:* contract
*Target and revision:* `tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents#306`, step 2b of #190, with the owner's step-2 decisions (https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/190#issuecomment-6243884276)
*Question:* The Issue review-and-draft round is prose: the parent composes every gate expectation and launch and supplies the Issue `contractInput` and `snapshotFingerprint` by hand (CL-D120). How does it run through packaged code without holding an Issue to the pull-request round's cost?
*Options and trade-offs:* A driver as strict as the pull-request one would require the package's authority files as gate evidence, bracket the snapshot, and carry the parent's preliminary revisions between gates, which makes every Issue round as expensive as a pull-request round. A thin driver keeps only what the gates need and stops for the owner instead.
*Recommendation:* The thin driver.
*Owner choice:* `skills/closed-loop-pr/driver/issue.js` runs one open Issue's review-and-draft round before CL-D31 candidate construction: Sol, then Terra (CL-D121), each launched by `build_gate_launch` from a `build_gate_expectation` expectation with the Issue correlation and each result read and validated by `gate_result_read`; the parent only makes the `subagent` calls it prints. The driver derives `contractInput` from the Issue Skill and the two shared references, and `snapshotFingerprint` as the SHA-256 of a fixed projection of one read of the Issue and every comment, bots included, which it computes itself as the pull-request driver computes its body identity, because the packaged snapshot shape is the pull request's. Each gate's required evidence is the Issue's `issue_spec` and snapshot identities only. Before Terra and at readiness, `issue_spec` is recomputed and a change stops the round at `BLOCKED`; a snapshot change alone reruns nothing. Any result other than `MERGE` stops the round at `WAITING_FOR_OWNER`, and the owner revises the Issue before a fresh run: CL-D85's recorded Minors are the pull-request root's, because on the Issue a reword changes the specification under review; an Issue without an `Acceptance criteria` section stops there before any gate; a pull request or a closed Issue is refused before any run; one identity check, on the first read and every re-read, requires the record to be this number's Issue in the requested repository (its name compared without case), so another number, kind or repository is refused before any run and stops a later round at `BLOCKED`; each run id is read once, a refused read included; Sol and Terra returning `MERGE` on the unchanged `issue_spec` ends at `IMPLEMENTATION_READY`. The round drafts a local review file with the Issue status block and publishes nothing. A stopped round is not resumed; a fresh run is the legacy resume. The driver aggregate alarm resets from 120,000 to 140,000 bytes for the new file. Step 3 of #190 (#308) names, in the Skill, the owner of each passage the driver or `build_gate_launch` performs (payload composition, `issue_spec`, the target kind, result reading and the status block) or scopes it to the candidate phase, and drops two sentences the parent does not act on (a duplicated resume sentence and the gates' required evidence, which this record states); the meaning of CL-D31 and CL-D32 is unchanged.
*Rationale:* The gates' cost is what an Issue round spends, so the driver adds no evidence, bracket or stage the gates do not need, and the owner's revision replaces the parent's preliminary one. Every gate is still composed, read and validated by the packaged helpers, and readiness still requires both formal gates on the current published specification.
*Validity and invalidation conditions:* Applies to the Issue review-and-draft round before candidate construction. The driver reads the `Acceptance criteria` section by a bounded grammar: an ATX heading of two to six `#` (closing `#`s allowed) outside fenced code (backtick or tilde fences indented up to three spaces, a backtick fence's opening line holding no other backtick), up to the next ATX heading, with `-`, `*`, `+`, `N.` or `N)` items; recognizing any other Markdown form is a change to this record. CL-D31/CL-D32 candidate construction, its gates and its publication stay the Skill's prose. Widening the required evidence, bracketing the snapshot, publishing from the driver, or moving the candidate route into it requires a new owner decision.
