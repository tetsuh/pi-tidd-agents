## CL-D125 — The packaged driver collects no separate enterprise policy
**Clauses:** CL-D125-record, CL-D125-driver

*Decision ID:* CL-D125
*Kind:* contract
*Target and revision:* `tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents#276`, the owner's choice recorded in that thread (2026-10-23)
*Question:* The exact-autofix precondition requires the head branch to be verified writable by a normal, non-force push, and a missing or unavailable writability result fails closed (CL-D30). `helpers/writability.js` refuses unless its caller proves that enterprise policy was collected completely, and the packaged driver passes that proof as a constant, so it never collects enterprise rulesets of its own. Should the driver record that assumption, or collect enterprise policy and fail closed when it cannot be established?
*Options and trade-offs:* (a) Record the assumption: a record states it, a clause pins the driver's call, and a test asserts that the call and the record agree. Nothing changes at run time. (b) Collect enterprise rulesets, or refuse when they cannot be established: the driver stops passing the proof as a constant, and a run on a repository whose enterprise policy is unreadable ends `BLOCKED` before any gate. That adds a collection path and a new refusal for a case GitHub's own push refusal already covers, after the gates have run.
*Recommendation:* (a).
*Owner choice:* (a). The packaged driver collects no enterprise policy of its own. The helper relies on GitHub's repository ruleset listing including the rulesets configured at higher levels that apply to the repository (its `includes_parents` default), so a ruleset that targets the repository from above appears in that listing and is evaluated like every other one. The driver's `writability` call passes `enterprisePolicyComplete: true, enterpriseRulesets: []`, which asserts that nothing applies beyond the repository and organization listings the helper reads. A listing or ruleset detail the actor cannot read stops collection, and the driver ends `BLOCKED` before any gate runs.
*Rationale:* The helper reads each ruleset the repository listing names, in detail, at the repository endpoint, and for an organization-owned repository the organization listing as well, and evaluates them all under the CL-D30 rule: active, applicable to the branch, bypass-free, and carrying only the rule types the helper knows to be safe. A ruleset configured above the repository that reaches the listing is therefore enforced exactly as a repository ruleset is; a detail that lacks its bypass actors or rules raises `invalid_schema`, and a listing or detail the `gh` transport cannot read raises `command_failed` (or `command_timeout`), both during collection, which the driver turns into `BLOCKED` before the first gate. The constant records that the driver supplies no ruleset those listings do not, and nothing more.
*Validity and invalidation conditions:* Applies to the packaged exact-autofix driver's writability preflight. It changes no rule of CL-D30, whose clauses still pin the helper's refusals and the call's prefix. Collecting enterprise policy separately, or supplying any ruleset outside the repository and organization listings, requires a new owner decision.
