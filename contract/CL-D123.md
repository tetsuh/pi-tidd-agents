## CL-D123 — The Issue root has no candidate publication route
**Clauses:** CL-D123-record, CL-D123-skill

*Decision ID:* CL-D123
*Kind:* contract
*Target and revision:* `tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents#310`, step 4 of #190, with the owner's decision (https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/190#issuecomment-6252295881)
*Question:* The post-merge verification of #190 step 3 raised `ADV-190-CANDIDATE-TRANSPORT`: the Issue Skill sent every CL-D31/CL-D32 candidate gate launch through `build_gate_launch`, whose volatile envelope has no field for the candidate, bundle or ledger. Should the builder carry the candidate, or should the route go?
*Options and trade-offs:* Extending the builder keeps the route, at the cost of an Issue-only candidate field, its end-to-end tests, and the candidate section, which was most of the Issue Skill and was read on every run. Since CL-D122 a gate finding stops the Issue round and the owner revises the Issue before a fresh run, so the candidate route, where the parent edits the Issue body and posts a ledger, duplicates that path.
*Recommendation:* Retire the route.
*Owner choice:* The Issue root is review-and-draft only: `/tidd-issue` and `/skill:closed-loop-issue` never edit the Issue or post to GitHub. When a round stops, the owner revises the Issue, and a fresh run reviews the revised text. The Issue Skill carries no candidate construction, preview, approval, publication attempt or snapshot-C proof, and its status block's `publication_grant` is always `not-applicable`. CL-D31 and CL-D32 become historical records whose clauses pin only their own files. `candidateIdentity` stays optional in gate-result schema v2 (CL-D120) and in the gate roles' output schemas, and the Issue root never sets it.
*Rationale:* The owner keeps Issue review lighter than pull-request review (issues/190#issuecomment-6243884276): the owner's own revision replaces the parent's candidate, and the Skill the parent reads on every run loses the route's text.
*Validity and invalidation conditions:* Applies to the Issue root. Removing `candidateIdentity` from the schemas is a separate schema change. Giving the Issue root any publication authority again requires a new owner decision.
