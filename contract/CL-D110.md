## CL-D110 — Review-only carries earlier rounds and stops after five without MERGE_READY
**Clauses:** CL-D110-record, CL-D110-driver, CL-D110-review-only, CL-D110-forward, CL-D110-tests

*Decision ID:* CL-D110
*Kind:* contract
*Target and revision:* `tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents#259` at its body, and the owner decision on it (https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/259#issuecomment-6091945569)
*Question:* CL-D11 bounds rounds per run, and every push starts a fresh run, so nothing bounded the runs of one pull request, and a fresh run's gates saw no disposition from earlier runs. tetsuh/hekatus PR #107 took fourteen rounds, each run opening at `convergence 1/3`, and a deferral the owner approved in run 11 did not reach run 12's gates. How does review-only account across fresh runs?
*Options and trade-offs:* Option A keeps runs independent, which is what produced the spiral. Option B reads the pull request's earlier round publications at start, carries their findings to the gates as settled, and stops after N rounds without `MERGE_READY` for an owner cut-off.
*Recommendation:* Option B.
*Owner choice:* Option B with N = 5. An earlier round is a conversation comment on the pull request by an `OWNER`, `MEMBER` or `COLLABORATOR` author that is not a bot, opens `# Review state: `, and carries a `tidd-status` block whose `target:` is this pull request; no publication marker is required, since a consumer may post the round by hand. Rounds after the latest `MERGE_READY` round, or after the latest trusted comment carrying the line `tidd-budget: continue`, are counted. Each finding line of a counted round reaches every gate's history as settled, with its disposition verbatim and the head the round names; a `needs-owner-decision` line is not carried. A finding settled this way is reopened only on materially new evidence, as the Every-gate block says; the defect still present at a later head is such evidence. At five counted rounds a run stops `WAITING_FOR_OWNER` before any validation or gate and names the count.
*Rationale:* The publications are already on the pull request and written by the owner's account, so reading them costs one listing; free text an owner adds outside the `tidd-status` block, such as a deferral note, is not read, and a deferral reaches the gates only as a finding's disposition.
*Validity and invalidation conditions:* Applies to the packaged review-only driver; exact autofix's accounting across fresh runs stays with #50. Changing N, reading a round from an untrusted author or a bot, or carrying a needs-owner-decision line requires a new owner decision.
