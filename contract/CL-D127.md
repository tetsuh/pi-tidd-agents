## CL-D127 — The autofix driver publishes a recorded reword in the non-carry form
**Clauses:** CL-D127-record, CL-D127-driver, CL-D127-driver-sites, CL-D127-forward, CL-D127-tests

*Decision ID:* CL-D127
*Kind:* contract
*Target and revision:* `tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents#326` at its body, the owner's choice of the recommended option
*Question:* CL-D126 gave the review-only driver a recorded-reword line the earlier-rounds reader does not carry and left the exact autofix driver's line to #50. #50 had closed before CL-D126 was written, with the owner's decision that exact-autofix runs carry no accounting, so nothing was scheduled to change that line. The autofix driver settles a Minor reword through CL-D85, fresh or assigned, and published it as `<disposition> (recorded under CL-D85)`, a line a later review-only run carries as settled although the autofix driver posts no correction comment. How does the autofix driver publish a recorded reword?
*Options and trade-offs:* Option 1 has the autofix driver post the reword's correction as a conversation comment: the correction would happen, but it adds a publication path to the writer's run. Option 2 gives the autofix line the review-only driver's non-carry form: nothing posts the correction, and the next run judges the claim afresh, so an uncorrected reword is listed again as a recorded Minor, never settled silently. Option 3 leaves the line and only rewrites the deferral.
*Recommendation:* Option 2.
*Owner choice:* Option 2. The autofix driver publishes a recorded reword, fresh or assigned, as `<disposition> (reword, recorded under CL-D85; judged afresh next run, CL-D126)`, the literal the review-only driver publishes, built by `recordedLine` in the shared phases module, so the earlier-rounds reader carries neither driver's reword line. A recorded finding that is not a reword keeps `<disposition> (recorded under CL-D85)`. The autofix driver posts no conversation comment; the publication carries `findingId: disposition` only, and the correction text stays in the run's state. No accounting is carried across autofix runs, and the review-only driver, the schema and the validator are unchanged.
*Rationale:* The carry is what made the old line wrong: it marked a claim settled across runs when nobody had narrowed or corrected it. The non-carry form keeps the finding visible until a trusted conversation comment settles it (CL-D108), which is the property CL-D126 chose for the review-only driver, and it adds no path that writes to the pull request.
*Validity and invalidation conditions:* Applies to the packaged exact autofix driver. Having the autofix driver post a conversation comment, carrying a recorded reword line as settled, or carrying accounting across autofix runs requires a new owner decision.
