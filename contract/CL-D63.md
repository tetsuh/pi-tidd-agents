## CL-D63 — Deterministic agreement checks are the first review layer
**Clauses:** CL-D63-payload, CL-D63-record, CL-D63-tests

*Decision ID:* CL-D63
*Kind:* contract
*Target and revision:* `tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents#110` at its body and the owner decision https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/110#issuecomment-5556555805; amended by the owner decision https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/pull/113#issuecomment-5584688495 (2026-09-08), which bounds the fence grammar of the checks
*Question:* PR #109 took nine adversarial rounds; nine of its eleven findings were surface-agreement gaps (a sentence, table, or fixture that still named only Sol and Terra, or kept a superseded restart phrase), each decidable without a model and each paid for at the formal gate. Where should that class of judgment be placed?
*Options and trade-offs:* Option A declares the role set, gate identities, gate order, status grammar, and restart phrase once, derives a literal for every declared prose surface from that source, denies phrases a decision has retired, and tells the adversarial gate that those surfaces are pre-checked. Option B keeps finding them through the adversarial gate, one per round. Option C adds a heuristic lint that decides whether an arbitrary sentence should mention a stage, which is the total classification CL-D44 recorded as failing under review and would become the kind of guard CL-D43 and CL-D48 removed.
*Recommendation:* Option A.
*Owner choice:* Option A. The source is `test/records/workflow-vocabulary.json`; the derived-vocabulary test checks every declared surface against it and rejects retired phrases everywhere except on a line carrying the recorded qualification. The boundary is part of the decision: validate declared surfaces with literals derived from one source; never classify prose; a denylist entry is added only when a decision retires a phrase, never speculatively. The Sol-only payload block states that pinned surfaces are pre-checked and are not re-raised, and that a surface-agreement gap found elsewhere is enumerated exhaustively in one result.
*Rationale:* A check a test can decide should not be bought from the agentic layer: the formal reviewer runs for #109 cost about seventy dollars, and the gate reports one Major per round, so N gaps cost N rounds. The derivation follows CL-D47 (a value copied by hand can only ever be wrong) applied to prose: the surfaces copy the source instead of restating it. The boundary follows CL-D44 (predicates on declared fields, no classification of open-world input) and CL-D43/CL-D48 (no proxy guard binding ordinary work), which is why the denylist is decision-driven and the checks name surfaces rather than scan sentences.
*Validity and invalidation conditions:* Applies to the declared surfaces the test enumerates and to the Sol-only payload duty. It changes no verdict vocabulary, gate authority, budget, or readiness rule, and grants the deterministic layer no verdict of its own: a pinned surface that drifts fails `npm test`, never a gate. Adding a heuristic classifier, a speculative denylist entry, or a guard that binds unrelated work requires a new owner decision. Fence grammar (amendment of 2026-09-08): the fence extractor recognizes CommonMark leaf fences: backtick or tilde runs of three or more, indented up to three spaces, closed by a same-character run at least as long, with nested fence content included; container prefixes such as block quotes and list items, and a general Markdown tokenizer, are outside the declared surface: the checks validate declared blocks and do not render Markdown (CL-D44); a finding that requires more Markdown grammar than this is not a CL-D63 defect and needs a new owner decision.
