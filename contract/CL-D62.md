## CL-D62 — A non-authoritative convergence stage runs before the adversarial gate
**Clauses:** CL-D62-shared, CL-D62-issue, CL-D62-pr, CL-D62-autofix, CL-D62-autofix-flow, CL-D62-autofix-map, CL-D62-readme, CL-D62-tests

*Decision ID:* CL-D62
*Kind:* contract
*Target and revision:* `tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents#101` at its body, the design proposal https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/101#issuecomment-5540926261, and the owner decision https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/101#issuecomment-5541436767
*Question:* The first formal adversarial gate pays the full Sol cost even when a cheaper reviewer would have found ordinary omissions first. How does a preliminary stage reduce Sol calls without becoming readiness authority, consuming the formal budgets, or looping on a model that will not settle?
*Options and trade-offs:* Five sub-questions were put to the owner with a recommendation each: the shipped default model (Luna, resolvable everywhere, versus an economical model that may not resolve); the budget (3 per run on the Issue root and review-only, 5 on exact autofix, one invocation per candidate identity and snapshot fingerprint, versus a fixed two); exhaustion (hand the candidate to the adversarial gate with open findings assigned, versus `ROUND_LIMIT_REACHED`); `NEEDS DECISION` (the ordinary owner-decision path, versus downgrading to a finding, which would put a gate-specific exception into the closed validator); and review-only behavior (stop at `WAITING_FOR_OWNER` before Sol on a preliminary `FIX BEFORE MERGE`, versus running Sol in the same run at full cost).
*Recommendation:* All five as recommended.
*Owner choice:* All five as recommended. `tidd-convergence-reviewer` is packaged as a read-only, fresh-context, non-authoritative role with default `gpt-5.6-luna` at `high` (CL-D87 later moved the shipped convergence default to `gpt-6-luna`), overridable per role, no alias; this decision widens the CL-D1 `agents/` freeze exactly once more, for that one file. The envelope gains the `convergence` identity under CL-D60 (schema version 2, both roots, namespace `CONV-`, no adversarial-results duty). Order per candidate identity and snapshot fingerprint: convergence, then adversarial, then decision-drift (Issue) or safety (PR). Rounds are accounted as `convergence <used>/<cap>` with a cap of 3 per run on the Issue root and PR review-only and 5 on exact autofix, one invocation per candidate identity and snapshot fingerprint, failures not consumed; at the cap the candidate goes to the adversarial gate with open convergence findings assigned. Attempts are keyed by public head and snapshot fingerprint (DEC-109-CONV-SNAPSHOT-001, Option B, https://github.com/tetsuh/pi-tidd-agents/issues/101#issuecomment-5549173659): on exact autofix, actionable snapshot evidence on an unchanged public head reruns convergence before Sol inside the cap of five, preserving CL-D30's same-head route to Sol. A disabled role skips the stage with `convergence: disabled`; an unresolved role or one with `edit`/`write` is `BLOCKED`. Every status block reports `resolved:` with each role's provider, model, and thinking level. Review-only stops at `WAITING_FOR_OWNER` on a preliminary `FIX BEFORE MERGE` before Sol runs. (CL-D85 later qualifies this for pull-request runs: a result whose only open findings are Minors recorded under CL-D85 advances to Sol instead of stopping.) On the Issue root the CL-D32 post-decision route runs convergence on the unchanged decision-containing candidate before Sol, and candidate-phase physical launch identities cover convergence, Sol, and Terra alike (ADV-109-CLD32-POST-DECISION-OMITS-CONVERGENCE). On the PR root, exact autofix's canonical flow starts at convergence and every correction or same-head evidence route returns there, and the packaged helper map's snapshot and `gate_result_validate` rows cover convergence, Sol, and Terra (ADV-109-EXACT-AUTOFIX-MAP-OMITS-CONVERGENCE). The exact-autofix and Issue-candidate reference transition models in the test fixtures route every correction and every new actionable evidence through convergence before Sol, hand over to Sol at the convergence cap, and reject the superseded direct-to-Sol transitions (ADV-109-STALE-REFERENCE-FIXTURES-RESTART-AT-SOL). The README's per-command preflight lists and its custom-definition guidance name the convergence role and the disable-to-skip rule (ADV-109-README-PREFLIGHT-OVERRIDE-OMITS-CONVERGENCE). The CL-D30 circuit breaker is shared with the stage: convergence observations count toward the `blockerKey × breakerOwner` no-progress history, one per result, so the third observation across convergence and formal gates stops the run while convergence stays outside the 15 counted invocations (ADV-109-CONVERGENCE-NO-PROGRESS-OMITTED). CL-D30's owner choice carries a living note that CL-D62 later inserts the convergence stage before Sol, superseding its direct post-push restart (ADV-109-CONTRACT-CLD30-RESTARTS-AT-SOL). Both root preflights require the convergence role unless it is explicitly disabled, with a missing, unresolved, or write-capable resolution `BLOCKED` (ADV-109-SKILL-PREFLIGHT-OMITS-CONVERGENCE); the convergence payload composes the Every-gate block and the volatile envelope only, the snapshot is refreshed before convergence as before Sol and Terra, and a candidate-changing convergence finding invalidates the candidate like a formal one.
*Rationale:* The stage reuses every existing mechanism instead of adding one: the CL-D36 envelope and validator with one more identity, the assigned-finding path that already carries round-N findings into round N+1, the existing disposition and owner-decision paths, the CL-D59 role and preflight rules, and the run-scoped accounting of CL-D11/CL-D12 with one more counter. Readiness cannot leak because only the unchanged formal gates emit it, and the stage cannot loop because it runs once per candidate identity and snapshot fingerprint and fails open toward the authority, never toward readiness. Luna as the shipped default keeps the package resolvable; the exact-autofix self-review that implies is a known limit recorded in the README and owned by #102.
*Validity and invalidation conditions:* Applies to both workflow roots' gate order, status blocks, and preflight, and to `gate_result_validate`'s identity set. It changes no formal gate's authority, budget, order relative to each other, verdict vocabulary, or confirmation duty, and grants no mutation in review-only mode. Changing the caps, the exhaustion rule, the default model, or making any preliminary outcome readiness-relevant requires a new owner decision; the worker-output-specific patch review is #102's, not this decision's.
